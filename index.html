90 day probation periods: what changes and what doesn't

In Germany it changes your legal position. In the US it mostly does not. The counting rule is the same everywhere and it is not three calendar months.

Updated September 2026  ·  Reading time about 4 minutes  ·  Topic: Employment and probation

Start date90-day windowDeadline

90 calendar days from your start date. Not three calendar months, and not working days.

On this page

    A 90 day probation period is the most common trial length in salaried employment worldwide. In some countries it changes your legal protection. In others it changes nothing at all and only exists because the contract template said so.

    What a probation period actually changes

    Three things, depending on where you are employed:

    • Notice. Shortened during probation, often to a week or two rather than the full period.
    • Benefits. Health cover, pension enrolment, bonus eligibility and some leave entitlements commonly start after confirmation.
    • Dismissal process. In systems with strong dismissal protection, the probation period is when that protection is lighter.

    What it does not change, anywhere, is your entitlement to be paid correctly, to accrue statutory leave, and to be protected against discrimination. Those apply from day one regardless of probation status, and employers who say otherwise are wrong.

    How the 90 days is counted

    Calendar days from your start date, not working days, and not three calendar months. Start on 1 March and the 90th day is 30 May, whereas three calendar months would be 1 June. Contracts that say "three months" and contracts that say "90 days" produce different dates in most starting months.

    Extensions are common and usually allowed if the contract says so. What the employer generally cannot do is extend it after it has already expired, because in many systems you become a confirmed employee automatically at the end of the stated period.

    Get confirmation in writing. In several countries, silence at the end of probation means you passed. That is useful, but a written confirmation letter is what you will want when applying for a mortgage, a visa or a loan, because lenders ask about probation status directly.

    What the first 90 days is actually assessed on

    Almost never on output alone. Managers deciding whether to confirm someone are answering three questions, usually without writing them down.

    Can they work without being managed closely

    The strongest signal in the first month is whether the manager has to check on you. Send a short weekly update before anyone asks for one, and the question answers itself.

    Do they take feedback once

    The same correction twice is the most commonly cited reason for failing probation in exit notes. Write feedback down when you get it.

    Do people want them in the room

    Unfair, but decisive. Confirmation decisions are social as much as technical, and the manager will ask two or three colleagues what they think.

    Ask for a review at day 30 and day 60 rather than waiting for day 90. A surprise at the end is a failure of process, and asking early puts any concern on the table while you still have two months to fix it.

    If probation is not confirmed

    You are entitled to the notice in your contract, to accrued holiday pay, and to your final pay on the normal schedule. You are not usually entitled to a redundancy payment, because this is a dismissal rather than a redundancy.

    Ask for the reason in writing and ask what the reference will say. Most employers will agree to a neutral reference covering dates and job title, and agreeing that in the exit conversation is far easier than negotiating it six months later.

    If you were dismissed for a reason connected to pregnancy, disability, race, religion, sex or whistleblowing, probation status does not protect the employer. Those claims do not need a qualifying period in the UK and have their own rules elsewhere.

    Other 90 day clocks

    Quarters run on 90 days, which is why business planning, OKR cycles and earnings reporting all use the same rhythm, and why the half-year point at 180 days is the other natural review date. Schengen visitors get 90 days in any 180 day period, a rolling count that catches people out because it is not a fresh allowance per visit. Many manufacturer warranties on parts and labour run 90 days. US employers sponsoring visas, and several immigration systems, use 90 days as the threshold for reporting a change of status.

    For employment specifically, the shorter one week notice often applies during this period, stepping up to a full contractual period only once probation is confirmed.

    Common questions

    Is 90 days the same as three months of probation?

    No. 90 calendar days from 1 March ends on 30 May, while three calendar months ends on 1 June. Check which wording your contract uses, because the confirmation date depends on it.

    Can an employer extend a 90 day probation?

    Usually yes if the contract allows it, and the extension has to be agreed before the original period expires. After expiry, many systems treat the employee as confirmed automatically.

    Do you get full employment rights during probation?

    You get statutory rights such as correct pay, leave accrual and protection from discrimination from day one. Contractual benefits like private health cover and full notice often start after confirmation.

    What notice applies during a probation period?

    Whatever the contract sets for probation, commonly one or two weeks rather than the full period. Statutory minimums still apply as a floor in countries that have them.
